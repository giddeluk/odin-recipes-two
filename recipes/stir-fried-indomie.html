Stir-fried Indomie

Description

Quick instant noodles upgraded Nigerian street-style by parboiling, then stir-frying in a hot pan with fresh chopped onions, peppers, and the savory seasoning packets for extra flavor and aroma.

Ingredients

  • 2 packs instant noodles (with seasoning packets)
  • 1 small onion & 1 pepper/tomato (chopped)
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • Water (for boiling)

Steps

  1. Parboil: Cook noodles in boiling water for 2 minutes, then drain completely.
  2. Sauté base: Heat oil in a pan, sauté chopped onion and pepper for 1 minute, and stir in seasoning packets.
  3. Toss noodles: Add drained noodles to the pan and toss continuously on low heat for 1 minute until well coated.
