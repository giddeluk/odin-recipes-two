Fried Plantain and Egg Sauce

Description

This classic Nigerian comfort combo pairs sweet, caramelised fried plantains (Dodo) and a savory, vegetable-packed scrambled egg sauce alongside spicy, stir-fried instant noodles. The contrast between the soft, naturally sweet plantains, the juicy seasoned eggs, and the savory peppered noodles creates a warm, delicious meal that comes together in under 20 minutes using just one main frying pan.

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe plantains (yellow with black spots for maximum sweetness)
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 small red onion (chopped)
  • 1 fresh tomato (chopped)
  • 1 scotch bonnet pepper (atarodo) or chili flakes (to taste, chopped)
  • 1 bouillon cube (or a pinch of salt and black pepper)
  • Vegetable oil(for frying)

Steps

  1. Fry Plantains: Fry plantain slices in hot oil for 2–3 minutes per side until golden; drain on paper towels.
  2. Sauté veggies: Pour out most oil, keeping 1 tbsp, and sauté the chopped tomato, onion, and pepper for 2 minutes.
  3. Scramble eggs: Pour in whisked eggs with seasoning, scramble gently until set, and serve alongside plantains.
